👁️ WATCH YOUR SURROUNDINGS

Confidence starts before an emergency. TACNET's safety guidance emphasizes awareness rather than confrontation.

  • Keep your head up and periodically scan behind and around you.
  • Avoid walking while fully absorbed in your phone.
  • At night, park near lighting and populated entrances.
  • If something feels wrong, move toward people, staff or a well-lit public location.
  • Trust your instincts. You do not owe a stranger a conversation.
  • If you believe you are being followed, do not lead the person to your home.
  • When driving, lock doors before leaving the parking space and keep keys ready.
